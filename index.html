Warship Compatibility Quiz

Which Cold War-era warship is your soul mate?

Have you ever wondered which finely-crafted naval weapon of war is your soul mate? No? Pffft, of course you have, don't be ridiculous.

Anyway, now you don't have to! Merely answer these objective and totally-not-loaded questions and let's discover which floating brick has the key to your heart!

After x amount of seconds, our top scientists from our best free-range asylums have found the warship that is meant for you! Are you ready?

USS Nimitz!

A Nimitz-class aircraft carrier. Displacing over 100,000 tons and over 1,000 feet long, the USS Nimitz is an absolute unit! She might never walk down the catwalk at New York Fashion Week, but she has all the space you need to start your comically oversized family, with plenty of hangar space to spare. While not the type of ship to end a fight, her massive amount of "kids" makes her presence known to all around her,even your sworn enemies. Built in 1975, she's still going strong today, and her speed of 32 knots makes her fast for her impressive size.

TAVKR Kiev!

The first Kiev-class aircraft carrier, although she'll tell you she's a "large aviation cruiser" (sure, whatever). Equipped with helicopters and light aircraft, she's great at digging up the tea on others, and she has plenty of ways to defend herself if that gets her into trouble. Built in 1975, she's a young and energetic gal, even if her modest aircraft means she won't easily engage in a fight. She can hold up to 30 aircraft, making her ideal to start your own little traditional Russian family. At 45,000 tons and 900 feet long, she's perfect for someone who wants a little more to hold onto, and her speed of 32 knots means she can both get shit done while also enjoying the little things in life.

USS Forrestal!

A Forrestal-class aircraft carrier who, being built in 1955, has all the age and experience you could ever want in a partner. Weighing over 60,000 tons and over 1,000 feet long, there's plenty of her to go around. At 33 knots, she's a speedy gal, and her huge "hangars" are perfect for all your personal and familial needs. Her large aircraft complement means she'll love to start a fight, even if she can't necessarily finish it.

PKR Leningrad!

A Moskva-class helicopter carrier that might get overlooked among the "big" and "important" supercarriers, but she's got a lot of heart. At *only* 12,000 tons and about 600 feet long, there's not too much to her, but being built in 1967, she has plenty of life experience with which to guide your relationship. She might not look the part of a Hollywood actress, but her hanger space would be great to start a small family. Not really built for fighting, either to start or end a conflict, and with a respectable if slower speed of 28 knots, she's perfect if you're looing for a quiet life with a partner who will always be there for you, even if she is never the life of the party. Be careful though, she's liable to fall over without warning, so always be on your guard!

TARKR Kirov!

The mighty Kirov! Lead ship of the Kirov-class large missile cruisers, she is the largest surface combatant built since WW2. Weighing in at a whopping 24,000 tons, and over 800 feet long, she's a big gal who knows how to get things going. With 20 long-range anti-ship missiles, and a range of self-defense weapons, she can start and end anything she wants, looking both dangerous and classy at the same time. First built in 1980, she's a newcomer to the dating scene, but her hull-mounter hanger can fit plenty of "kids" and her speed of 32 knots means she can quickly duck and cover if things get a little too spicy. But it's Kirov. She loves it when it's spicy.

RKR Slava!

The first Slava-class missile cruiser, and coming in at 9,300 tons, she's a little thicc compared to most of the other guys/ gals around, but is always in her big sister Kirov's shadow. And no, she isn't self-conscious about it, thank you very much. Her 16 Sandbox anti-ship missiles really aren't easy on the eyes, but she's determined to prove that she can get a party started just as well as her big sister. And with an array of defensive weapons and a 32 knot speed, she can also resolve sticky situations if the need arises. Built in 1982, it'd be hard to find someone younger than her, and her small hanger space means she'll never want a large family.

BPK Nikolayev!

Graceful yet formidable, this Kara-class cruiser is the jack of all trades in the warship dating scene. At 8,200 tons and 560 feet long, her size and length aren't particularly noteworthy, but her 34 knot top speed and single hanger means she can start a family and keep it going through all of life's twists and turns. Like the gorgeous cashier at your local supermarket, she might not look like she'll get into hot water, but her array of weapons, both offensive and defensive, might surprise you! First built in 1971, she's just the right age for anyone who doesn't want someone too inexperienced or too old to be fun.

BPK Otvazhny!

This Kashin-class destroyer is ready for an adventure! First built in 1962, she's been around the block a few times, and isn't here to play games. At 470 feet but only 3,400 tons, she's perfect if you're looking for someone tall and skinny! At 34 knots, she knows where she wants to go in life, and there's no room for kids in a relationship with her. Otherwise, she'll mostly keep to herself, but soon you'll notice how beautiful she is and just how dependable she can be, even if she's quietly supporting her friends and family in the background. A cute little ship that you can always count on, what's not to love?

EM Besstrashny!

Besstrashny means fearless, and let me tell you, this maverick has no fear! One of the Sovremenny-class destroyers, this young thing is coming out swinging, and good luck if you get in her way! At *only* 6,600 tons and just over 500 feet long, she might seem unremarkable, but her deadly Sunburn anti-ship missiles would make even a Kirov or Ticonderoga quake in their stern! Add onto that an instinctive ability to defend herself thanks to a top-of-the-line missile system and a top speed of 32 knots, and you've found a partner who can punch well above her weight. Combined with her flattering twin 5-inch guns and a style like she models for Calvin Klein on the side, she will sweep you off your feet from the moment you are blessed enough to lay eyes on her. First built in 1980, she has plenty of fight still in her, too.

USS Iowa!

When you hear "battleship," you think of Iowa! The first Iowa-class battleship, Iowa is as old-school as can be! First built in 1943, she was considered old when most Cold War warships hadn't even been designed. At 890 feet long and displacing 48,000 tons, calling her a big girl would be an understatement, and her 16-inch guns make a statement both on the streets and in the sheets.
