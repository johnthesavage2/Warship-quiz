Warship Compatibility Quiz

Which Cold War-era warship is your soul mate?

Have you ever wondered which finely-crafted naval weapon of war is your soul mate? No? Pffft, of course you have, don't be ridiculous.

Anyway, now you don't have to! Merely answer these objective and totally-not-loaded questions and let's discover which floating brick has the key to your heart!

After x amount of seconds, our top scientists from our best free-range asylums have found the warship that is meant for you! Are you ready?

USS Nimitz!

A Nimitz-class aircraft carrier. Displacing over 100,000 tons and over 1,000 feet long, the USS Nimitz is an absolute unit! She might never walk down the catwalk at New York Fashion Week, but she has all the space you need to start your comically oversized family, with plenty of hangar space to spare. While not the type of ship to end a fight, her massive amount of "kids" makes her presence known to all around her,even your sworn enemies. Built in 1975, she's still going strong today, and her speed of 32 knots makes her fast for her impressive size.

TAVKR Kiev!

The first Kiev-class aircraft carrier, although she'll tell you she's a "large aviation cruiser" (sure, whatever). Equipped with helicopters and light aircraft, she's great at digging up the tea on others, and she has plenty of ways to defend herself if that gets her into trouble. Built in 1975, she's a young and energetic gal, even if her modest aircraft means she won't easily engage in a fight. She can hold up to 30 aircraft, making her ideal to start your own little traditional Russian family. At 45,000 tons and 900 feet long, she's perfect for someone who wants a little more to hold onto, and her speed of 32 knots means she can both get shit done while also enjoying the little things in life.

USS Forrestal!

A Forrestal-class aircraft carrier who, being built in 1955, has all the age and experience you could ever want in a partner. Weighing over 60,000 tons and over 1,000 feet long, there's plenty of her to go around. At 33 knots, she's a speedy gal, and her huge "hangars" are perfect for all your personal and familial needs. Her large aircraft complement means she'll love to start a fight, even if she can't necessarily finish it.

PKR Leningrad!

A Moskva-class helicopter carrier that might get overlooked among the "big" and "important" supercarriers, but she's got a lot of heart. At *only* 12,000 tons and about 600 feet long, there's not too much to her, but being built in 1967, she has plenty of life experience with which to guide your relationship. She might not look the part of a Hollywood actress, but her hanger space would be great to start a small family. Not really built for fighting, either to start or end a conflict, and with a respectable if slower speed of 28 knots, she's perfect if you're looing for a quiet life with a partner who will always be there for you, even if she is never the life of the party.
